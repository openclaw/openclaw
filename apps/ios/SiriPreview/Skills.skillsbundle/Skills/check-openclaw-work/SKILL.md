---
name: check-openclaw-work
description: Check existing OpenClaw work or open its conversation without sending another task.
---

Use Check OpenClaw Status for a progress question about a selected session.
Clarify an ambiguous session before checking. Do not use Ask OpenClaw to poll.
The status describes the session, not the success of a particular task. Unknown
activity does not mean idle, and no active work does not mean successful completion.
Use Open Session when the person wants to inspect the conversation itself.
Do not read out message content that the status action did not return.
