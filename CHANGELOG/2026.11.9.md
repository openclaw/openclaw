### Fixes

- **Command, channel, and web boundaries:** Keep `env -S` payload assignments visible to blocked-key and shell-startup guards, apply current Discord DM pairing and allowlist policy before using a bound session, and confine self-hosted web endpoint redirects to the configured host. (#203934, #196848, #196847) Thanks @pgondhi987 and @drobison00.
- **Claude CLI and ACP delivery:** Retain secret input pipes through asynchronous child readiness and deliver the complete final reply to ACP clients whose prompt was queued behind other work. (#202646, #202658) Thanks @obviyus.
- **Provider requests and prompt caching:** Keep callable Tool Search history native on chat-completions providers, reuse the parent prompt cache for OpenAI session forks, and stop Anthropic forks from caching their own suffix. (#202436, #197226, #197224) Related #202415 Thanks @obviyus and @paulsimmondsplato.
- **Model configuration:** Keep configured model rows, including explicit thinking-level entries, unchanged when you sign in to a provider again. (#201742) Related #201558 Thanks @MertBasar0, @obviyus, and @Caitoz.
- **Browser screenshots:** Keep screenshots working for background tabs in headed Chrome instead of timing out and blocking later captures. (#202878) Related #165312 Thanks @obviyus and @titan550.
