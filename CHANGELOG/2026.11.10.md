#### Highlights

##### Deep Research

Tackle questions that need more than a quick search without leaving your conversation. Start a research run in chat, follow its progress live, and open the underlying sources to verify every claim yourself.
When sources disagree, the portable research engine surfaces quoted passages side by side and flags gaps in the evidence so you can judge the answer directly. You can leave the chat and return to the finished report later, eligible jobs can resume after a Gateway restart, and reports are retained for seven days.

##### Knowledge

Ask your agent why a project changed direction and trace the answer straight back to the meeting or message where you made the call. Knowledge is OpenClaw’s bundled, opt-in memory plugin, turning scattered conversations, notes, and files into a linked vault of people, projects, decisions, and sources you can browse, question, and correct over time.
**Build a project history from the work itself.** Hand your agent a document, article, or conversation and ask it to capture what matters. It creates linked pages for people, organizations, and projects, records decisions and commitments with supporting excerpts, and keeps original files on hand to open later. A decision made in a meeting can appear on the project page alongside the people involved, linked directly back to that meeting.

**Let scribes keep it current.** Start a scribe to learn from OpenClaw conversations or, when Granola is connected, from your meetings, either as a one-time catch-up or on a schedule. Another scribe can bring in existing memory notes and the Dreams diary without altering the originals, while scheduled consolidation organizes loose claims and updates pages as decisions turn into outcomes.

**Find the evidence and bring it into chat.** In the Knowledge explorer, search by keyword, name, alias, or date, follow links between records, or open a project’s Timeline to review its dated sources. Searching under an old name still leads you to the current record, and asking your agent about a page anchors the conversation to that version. Optional automatic recall can surface relevant passages in new conversations, starting in shadow mode so you can inspect matches before turning it live. Even in shadow mode, keyword scoring sends your message and candidate excerpts to the configured Decision provider.

**Correct the record without erasing history.** Ask your agent to revise a claim, withdraw an outdated one, or merge duplicate pages while keeping earlier versions and their supporting evidence inspectable. Records live in local Markdown files, and when Git is available with writes enabled, the vault maintains local Git checkpoints.

**Choose who shares the context.** Enable Knowledge by selecting it as your memory provider. Each agent gets its own store by default, or several agents can share one, and switching to Knowledge leaves your existing memory data untouched. The [Knowledge guide](https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/knowledge) covers [stores and access](https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/knowledge/stores-and-access), [scribes](https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/knowledge/scribes), [search](https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/knowledge/retrieval), and [corrections](https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/knowledge/corrections).

##### Browser handoff

When your agent hits a form or sign-in flow you would rather complete yourself, step in and take over the tab. Hand control back when you finish and the agent picks up right where you left off.
Choose **Take control** on a supported Gateway-hosted Browser tab to pause the agent’s clicks and typing on that tab while you work. The agent can still take screenshots or work elsewhere, because the handoff changes who can act on the page rather than who can see it. Release control whenever you are ready, or let it return automatically after five minutes without input.

[#197928](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/197928).

##### Agent Templates in Labs

  Agent Templates remains behind **Settings -> Labs -> Templates** and is off by default. #### Messaging

You can now fork threaded conversations into independent siblings across Telegram, Discord, and Slack, link your own Discord account from your personal profile, and rely on steadier progress cards, formatting, and durable delivery across channels.


##### Fork threaded conversations in Telegram, Discord, and Slack


You can branch an existing channel thread into an independent sibling conversation with `/fork [title]`. OpenClaw copies the current conversation snapshot into a new ordinary session, keeps the original thread on its existing session, and posts a short literal recap of up to three recent visible text messages in the new thread with mentions and link previews disabled. Hidden reasoning and tool output are never posted, and a busy thread forks through its last completed turn rather than a partial answer.

Each platform keeps its own native boundaries. Telegram creates a sibling forum topic in the same supergroup when thread bindings are enabled and the bot has Manage Topics. Discord creates a sibling public thread, private thread, or forum post under the same parent channel, keeping private threads non-invitable and adding only the requester. Slack creates a new top-level root in the same channel when you send `@OpenClaw /fork [title]` as an ordinary message inside an existing channel thread. Direct messages, unthreaded channels, and Enterprise Grid org installs are not supported, and OpenClaw never retries creation automatically after an uncertain outcome so you can inspect the parent channel before trying again.
Control UI **Fork conversation** shares the same session-copy operation without inserting a duplicate recap. Forking does not clone the workspace or undo file or external effects.

[#190783](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/190783), [#190785](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/190785), [#190786](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/190786).

##### Opt-in Knowledge recall gate with reranker support


Operators can opt into a per-turn, model-scored recall gate under `plugins.entries.knowledge.config.recallGate` that scores shortlisted Knowledge pages through the agent's configured Decision model before the main model answers. A new bundled Rerank provider connects to any `/rerank` endpoint such as vLLM, Hugging Face TEI, Infinity, llama.cpp, Cohere, Jina, or Voyage.

The gate is off until configured and starts in `shadow` mode for logging and threshold tuning before you switch to `live` injection. Both shadow and live modes send the user message and candidate excerpts to the configured Decision provider, and live mode injects model-selected page text rather than owner-approved summaries, so use a provider you trust with private vault content.
Existing owner-approved `knowledge_recall` summaries and the standing index remain unchanged. Unavailability, errors, or timeouts skip the optional injection.

#### Browser and Computer Use

You can now take temporary control of the agent's browser from the Browser panel, open Chrome settings and password manager tabs privately out of the agent's reach, try the experimental Cloudflare Kitesurf engine, and rely on steadier managed browser sessions.


##### Take control of the agent's browser from the Browser panel


You can click **Take control** in the Control UI Browser panel toolbar to solve a CAPTCHA, finish a sign-in, or edit a form without the agent acting over you. While you hold control, browser actions from the agent's browser tool, the `openclaw browser` CLI, and other Control UI windows are refused, and the agent receives a normal result explaining that you have taken over so its turn can continue. The agent can still take snapshots and screenshots while you hold control, and an action that had already started may finish.

Click **Release control** to hand the browser back. Control also releases automatically when the Control UI window closes or disconnects, or after five minutes without deliberate input from you. **Take control** is available for browsers on the Gateway host, not for node-routed browsers, and ordinary clicks or typing without clicking **Take control** leave the agent's browser results unchanged. The Browser panel also cuts typing lag and screencast stutter so interactive sessions feel responsive.
[#197928](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/197928), [#198454](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/198454), [#197927](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/197927).

Thanks [@daniel-lxs](https://github.com/daniel-lxs).




##### Open Chrome settings and password manager privately in the Browser panel


You can now open `chrome://settings` (such as `chrome://settings/payments`) and `chrome://password-manager` from the Browser panel address bar to manage payment cards or saved passwords yourself. Unlike **Take control**, these pages are isolated from the agent completely. They always open in a new tab accessible only to your authenticated Control UI connection, and the agent's browser tool, the `openclaw browser` CLI, and other Gateway clients cannot open, list, read, capture, or interact with them even while you have one open. Other `chrome://` pages remain blocked for everyone.

This works for locally managed and CDP-attached Chromium profiles on the Gateway host. It is not available for node-routed browsers, Chrome MCP existing-session profiles, the browser extension, or headless-shell profiles. If Chrome asks for your device password before revealing a saved password, that prompt appears on the computer running the browser rather than inside the panel.
[#197904](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/197904).

Thanks [@daniel-lxs](https://github.com/daniel-lxs).




##### Experimental Cloudflare Kitesurf browser engine


You can attach OpenClaw's browser tool to an externally managed [Cloudflare Kitesurf](https://github.com/cloudflare/kitesurf) CDP endpoint by configuring a profile with `engine: "kitesurf"` and `attachOnly: true`. The adapter supports navigation, tabs, text, ARIA snapshots, semantic clicks, typing, form filling, evaluation, and experimental rendered screenshots without running a Chromium process.

OpenClaw does not install, launch, or deploy Kitesurf, and targets last only for the active CDP connection, so do not use this profile to preserve logins. PDF export, downloads, uploads, dialogs, emulation, logs, visual actions, and action batches remain Chromium-only, and any deployed Kitesurf endpoint must be protected with your own authentication because upstream ships without built-in auth.
[#198833](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/198833).

Thanks [@vincentkoc](https://github.com/vincentkoc).
