- **Scheduled jobs:** Handle queued cron storage failures without exiting the Gateway, and clear one-shot cleanup when a job becomes recurring. (#175678, #168985) Authored by @steipete. Thanks @yxshee, @wangmiao0668000666, and @obviyus.
- **Updates:** Let profile-backed updates recognize their own maintenance lock and preserve cancellation when ledger writes are deferred. (#177792, #187998) Thanks @GrahamMcBain and @vincentkoc.
- **Update recovery:** Recover safely when the initial package-presence check times out, requiring a fresh valid directory identity before replacement and retaining rollback to the original package. (#181906) Authored by @steipete.
- **Anthropic usage caps:** Recognize exhausted plural usage-limit errors without spending the transient rate-limit retry budget. (#176186; related #176179) Thanks @sunlit-deng and @blue-dot-1.
- **Terminal sessions:** Forward /reset soft to the Gateway instead of hard-resetting the session. (#172431) Thanks @yetval and @obviyus.
- **Git recovery:** Preserve uncertain command failures during Git repair rather than treating an unconfirmed operation as successful. (#176006) Authored by @steipete.
