# Company page layout

A company page answers: what does it do, how does it relate to the user's work,
what is the state of that relationship, and who are the contacts? State covers
what they do, stage and key numbers. Use these sections after State, in this
order, and only those the evidence supports. Follow the general rules in
[subject pages](profiles.md), including the default of no change.

- `## Relationship`: how it relates to the user's work (partner, sponsor,
  vendor, customer, competitor) and the state of any agreement, with amounts and
  dates as the sources state them.
- `## People`: contacts and their roles.
- `## Engagements`: active joint work, linking each project page.
- `## Open Threads`: pending items, with owners and dates when known.

Joint work with its own history belongs on its project page, linked from
Engagements, not narrated here.
