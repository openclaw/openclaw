# Project page layout

A project page answers: what is it for, where does it stand, what shaped it,
who is involved, and what is due or undecided? Use these sections after State,
in this order, and only those the evidence supports. Follow the general rules
in [subject pages](profiles.md), including the default of no change.

- `## Key decisions`: decisions that still shape the project, newest first, one
  bullet each: what was decided, who decided, when, and why. A decision a later
  one replaced drops off; its claim keeps the history.
- `## Milestones`: significant outcomes and targets with their dates: launched,
  released, merged, deployed, and upcoming target dates. Not meetings, reviews,
  CI runs or interim pull-request status, and not a log of reports.
- `## People`: who leads it, who works on it and who has a stake, with each
  person's role on this project.
- `## Commitments and deadlines`: open commitments with owner and due date when
  known. A finished commitment moves to Milestones or drops off.
- `## Open questions`: unresolved decisions and risks, with who owns each.
- `## Related`: parent project, workstream pages and closely related pages, one
  line each.

State says where things stand. These sections hold the specifics State would
otherwise accumulate, so a fact belongs in one place, not both. A workstream,
component or phase of the project stays a State topic unless it qualifies as
its own page ([splitting](profiles.md#the-default-is-no-change)).
